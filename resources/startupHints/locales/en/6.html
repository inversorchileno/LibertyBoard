Spotlight / Magnifier

So that everyone looks at one place.

  • Spotlight: darkens the screen except a circle around the pointer (Radius and Darkness in the bar).
  • Magnifier: enlarges what is under the pointer.
  • The Spotlight and Magnifier buttons turn them on, change from one to the other and turn them off.
The icon of the tool palette only chooses the tool; it turns on with its buttons.

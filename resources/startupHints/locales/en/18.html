Desktop mode

Write and draw over any open program.

  • You go in with Desktop (top right); the first button of the palette goes back to the board.
  • The triangles on the buttons show their options.
  • The AI works with a part of the screen you choose, and what it brings stays over the desktop.
  • The red triangle makes the palette small; the bin erases everything drawn.
  • A colored border reminds you that you are in the desktop mode (Preferences).
The palette is upright at the left and right edges, and lies across at the top or bottom.

Laser pointer

To point at things in a presentation without leaving anything written.

  • Trail: leaves a stroke that fades. Point: only a red dot. Follow: follows the mouse without clicking.
  • Effects: Standard, White burn and Rainbow.
  • Opacity, Wait (how long the trail stays) and Fade (how long it takes to go).
  • Width and color as with the pen.
The laser also works on a locked canvas.

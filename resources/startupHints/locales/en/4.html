Marker and eraser

The marker paints over the text without hiding it; the eraser removes what was drawn.

  • Marker: round tip (round ends) or straight tip (like a highlighter, wide over the lines).
  • Marker: color, width and transparency.
  • Whole eraser: erases the whole stroke you touch.
  • Partial eraser: erases only where you pass, of the size chosen.
In the desktop mode, the bin of the palette erases everything drawn on the screen.

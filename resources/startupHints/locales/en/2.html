The tool palette

Each button chooses a tool, and its options appear in the bar at the top.

  • In the Customize menu you choose which buttons are shown, on the board and on the desktop.
  • With Customize → Move items you change their order by dragging them.
  • A button taken out keeps its tool in the Tools menu.
  • Each tool has a keyboard shortcut: see and change them in About → Shortcuts.
The tools that do two things, like Spotlight / Magnifier, change from one to the other in their options bar.

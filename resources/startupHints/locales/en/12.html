Text

To write with the keyboard.

  • Click on the page and write; font, size, bold, italic and color in the bar.
  • Check boxes, bullets and numbers.
  • Table: asks for rows and columns; Tab goes to the next cell; the lines are dragged.
  • Dictate: writes what you say (the dictation of Windows, Windows + H).
  • Transcribe: writes what is said in an audio, with an AI.
The microphone at the left of the F in each text box also dictates.

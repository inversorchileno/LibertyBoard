Shapes and arrow

Perfect figures and arrows to explain.

  • 20 shapes: line, polyline, ellipse, rectangle, triangles, rhombus, block arrows, stars, speech bubbles and cloud.
  • Polyline: a click at each corner; any key ends it and Escape drops it.
  • Border and fill with their color, width, line style and opacity.
  • Arrow: line style, endings and the tapered arrow, which grows towards its tip.
The Shapes icon shows the shape chosen.
